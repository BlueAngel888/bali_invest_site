About the area

A creative city on the black-sand coast

Nuanu is a master-planned creative city in Tabanan, on Bali's west coast, about 15 minutes from Canggu. It mixes villas, residences and hotels with beach clubs, art spaces, international schools, wellness and restaurants, all inside one walkable, landscaped community next to Nyanyi Beach.

Every project on this page sits inside Nuanu and uses its shared infrastructure. Several offer freehold ownership for foreign buyers.
